## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Refund Policy (the “Policy”) explains how Operava Global Solutions Opc, corporation organized under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines (“Operava,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), handles refund requests, cancellations, service credits, service-quality concerns, billing errors, payment disputes, and related matters.

This Policy applies to Products and Services that Operava offers, sells, licenses, delivers, manages, supports, or makes available through digital, remote, online, onsite, project-based, subscription-based, recurring, or other commercial arrangements, including IT development, BPO, staffing, SaaS, digital products, and related services.

This Policy is intended to be consistent with applicable Philippine law. It does not limit rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded, waived, or limited.

## 2. Company and Service Overview

Operava is a Philippines-based information technology and business process outsourcing company. Operava provides Services to Clients in the Philippines and internationally. Operava does not provide services to offshore gaming activities and will conduct its operations only within the limits permitted by applicable Philippine law.

## 7–10. Eligibility, Full/Partial Refunds, Service Credits

Refund eligibility includes duplicate charges, billing errors, unauthorized payments, non-commencement after valid cancellation, material service deficiency not cured, non-conforming deliverables, express contractual refunds, prepaid unperformed services, or mandatory legal requirements. Full and partial refunds and service credits are assessed case-by-case. Service credits expire twelve (12) months after issue unless otherwise stated.

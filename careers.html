Open positions

Three standard professional positions

1. OPERAVA Technology Executive

Broad technology position · Remote-first · Client-assigned · Global

General role

A broad technology position supporting clients and OPERAVA operations across IT, software, digital platforms, cloud, systems, applications, and related technology services.

Possible specializations / assignments

  • Information Technology, IT Support, Technical Support
  • Cloud Services, Cloud Operations, Cloud Infrastructure Support
  • Web Development, Web Application Development, Mobile Application Development
  • Software Development, Computer Programming, SaaS and Platform Development
  • Application Support, IT Systems Support, Systems Integration, API Support
  • Database Support, Data Technology, DevOps support
  • Other related technology functions

Minimum qualifications

Pathway A — At least 2 years relevant experience; demonstrable competency; pass OPERAVA assessment.

Pathway B — College degree or equivalent in a relevant field; at least 1 year experience; pass OPERAVA assessment.

Apply — Technology Executive

2. OPERAVA Customer Service Executive

Broad customer-service position · Remote-first · Client-assigned · Global

General role

A broad customer-service position supporting clients through customer communication, assistance, account support, and related customer operations.

Possible specializations / assignments

  • Voice Customer Service, Chat Support, Email Support
  • Customer Care, Customer Support, Account Assistance
  • Order Support, Service Inquiries, Customer Follow-up
  • Basic Customer Troubleshooting, Customer Experience Support

Minimum qualifications

Pathway A — At least 2 years relevant customer-service, BPO, or related experience; pass OPERAVA assessment.

Pathway B — College degree or equivalent; at least 1 year relevant experience; pass OPERAVA assessment.

Apply — Customer Service Executive

3. OPERAVA Business Operations Executive

Broad business-operations position · Remote-first · Client-assigned · Global

General role

A broad business-operations position supporting clients through administrative, back-office, data, and business-process functions.

Possible specializations / assignments

  • HR Operations, Human Resources Support
  • Accounting Support, Finance Operations Support
  • Data Processing, Data Entry, Back-Office Operations
  • Administrative Operations, Document Processing, Virtual Assistance

Minimum qualifications

Pathway A — At least 2 years relevant experience; pass OPERAVA assessment.

Pathway B — College degree or equivalent; at least 1 year experience; pass OPERAVA assessment.

Apply — Business Operations Executive

Process

Hiring & selection

Step 1 — Initial / AI interview

Applicants may complete an initial AI-assisted interview. Passing does not guarantee employment.

Step 2 — OPERAVA Talent Acquisition interview

Progressing applicants may be interviewed by OPERAVA Talent Acquisition.

Step 3 — Client interview

Client interview may be required before assignment to a particular client position.

Assessment

Applicants must pass the applicable OPERAVA assessment. Client-specific assessments may also apply.

Model

Client assignment & remote work

OPERAVA’s hiring model is skills-based and client-assignment driven. Roles are remote-first and open globally where legally permitted.

Employment disclaimer. Posting a position does not guarantee employment, a specific client assignment, specialization, schedule, or workload.

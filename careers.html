Now Hiring — Standard Professional Positions

OPERAVA is building a growing remote workforce to support clients across technology, business operations, customer service, and other digitally enabled services. Remote-first · Client-assigned · Skills-based work.

Important position disclaimer. These positions are intentionally designed as broad, generalist professional titles. Not every applicant or employee will perform every task listed. Actual responsibilities depend on client requirements, the specific service or project, education, experience, demonstrated skills, assessment results, training, and assigned specialization. Listed functions are examples of possible assignments and are not a promise that every employee will perform all of them.

Open positions

Three standard professional positions

1. OPERAVA Technology Executive

Broad technology position · Remote-first · Client-assigned

General role

A broad technology position supporting clients and OPERAVA operations across information technology, software, digital platforms, cloud, systems, applications, and related technology services.

Possible specializations / assignments

Depending on qualifications and client requirements, assignments may include:

  • Information Technology, IT Support, Technical Support
  • Cloud Services, Cloud Operations, Cloud Infrastructure Support
  • Web Development, Web Application Development, Mobile Application Development
  • Software Development, Computer Programming, SaaS and Platform Development
  • Application Support, IT Systems Support, Systems Integration, API Support
  • Database Support, Data Technology, DevOps support
  • Digital technology operations, technical documentation, technology implementation
  • Other related technology functions

General responsibilities

The employee will perform only the functions applicable to their assigned specialization. Responsibilities may include:

  • Perform assigned technology tasks according to client requirements
  • Develop, maintain, configure, support, or operate technology systems within the employee’s area of competency
  • Troubleshoot assigned technical issues
  • Follow technical procedures and documentation
  • Maintain accurate technical records
  • Support testing and implementation activities
  • Communicate technical issues clearly
  • Escalate issues outside the employee’s authority or competency
  • Follow information-security and confidentiality requirements
  • Maintain professional communication with clients and team members
  • Participate in training and continuous skills development

Minimum qualifications

Pathway A — Undergraduate / non-degree pathway

  • At least 2 years of relevant work experience in the applicable technology specialization
  • Demonstrable technical competency
  • Must successfully complete OPERAVA’s required assessment

Pathway B — College graduate pathway

  • At least a college degree, diploma, or recognized higher-education qualification relevant to the assigned specialization
  • At least 1 year of relevant work experience
  • Demonstrable competency in the applicable technology
  • Must successfully complete OPERAVA’s required assessment

Examples of relevant fields may include Information Technology, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Information Systems, Web Development, Programming, data-related fields, and other relevant technology disciplines.

General qualifications include professionalism, clear verbal and written communication, active listening, customer-service mindset, basic computer literacy, ability to follow procedures, problem-solving ability, reliability, confidentiality, and ability to work remotely.

Process

Hiring & selection

Step 1 — Initial / AI interview

Applicants may first complete an initial AI-assisted interview to evaluate general suitability, communication, experience, basic qualifications, and role alignment. Passing the initial AI interview does not guarantee employment.

Step 2 — OPERAVA Talent Acquisition interview

Applicants who progress may be interviewed by the OPERAVA Talent Acquisition team on experience, education, professionalism, communication, work history, skills, remote-work readiness, position alignment, availability, and general suitability.

Step 3 — Client interview

Applicants selected for a client assignment may proceed to a client interview evaluating technical or functional competency, relevant experience, communication, role-specific knowledge, client-specific requirements, and cultural and operational fit.

Assessment requirement

Applicants must successfully pass the applicable OPERAVA assessment after the Initial / First AI Interview and before progressing through the applicable hiring stages. The assessment may vary by position and specialization.

Model

Client assignment & remote work

OPERAVA’s hiring model is skills-based and client-assignment driven. Being hired under one of the three broad positions does not mean the employee will perform every function listed. Actual work is determined by qualifications, experience, assessment, training, client requirements, and performance.

OPERAVA operates remote-first. Applicants should be prepared to work remotely, use approved digital channels, maintain reliable connectivity, protect confidential information, and meet assigned schedules.

Career progression: Generalist → Specialist → Senior Specialist → Subject Matter Expert, based on performance, skills, training, and business needs.

Employment disclaimer. Posting a position does not guarantee employment, a specific client assignment, specialization, schedule, or workload. Actual terms are set in employment and client-assignment documentation. Licensed or regulated functions require the applicable qualification before performance.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers on partnering with Operava

Everything you need to know about our operating model, talent vetting, security, and commercial agreements.

How quickly can an engineering pod or support team be deployed?
Standard placements and agile pods are typically assembled and onboarded within 10 to 14 business days. For specialized architectural skill sets or large multi-tier BPO ramps, we provide a concrete ramp-up schedule during the initial 48-hour discovery scoping.
How does Operava ensure intellectual property and client data security?
All engagements are protected by comprehensive bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) and Master Services Agreements that explicitly assign 100% of work product and IP to the client. Our personnel operate on secured, monitored endpoints adhering to the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
What timezone overlaps are available for international clients?
We offer customized shift structures guaranteeing 4–6 hours of direct working overlap with North American (PST, CST, EST) and European (GMT, CET) business hours, ensuring seamless participation in your daily standups, sprint plannings, and release cycles.
What are the billing cycles and commercial contract options?
We offer flexible commercial arrangements: dedicated monthly talent retainers, milestone-based Statements of Work (SOWs), and volume-based managed BPO agreements. Invoices are issued with transparent net terms and accepted via global wire or corporate payment rails.
What is Operava's policy regarding refunds, cancellations, and service credits?
Operava maintains a transparent, comprehensive 49-section Refund Policy. While work already performed and accepted is non-refundable, we provide replacement guarantees for personnel during the initial 14-day window, service credits for SLA remediation, and full refunds for unperformed prepaid retainers upon lawful cancellation.

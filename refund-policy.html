Refund Policy

Authoritative refund rules, cancellation conditions, service credits, billing remedies, and resolution frameworks for Operava Global Solutions.

Entity Operava Global SolutionsEffective August 25, 2026Last updated August 25, 2026Jurisdiction Republic of the Philippines

Important. Cancellation is not automatically a refund. Nothing in this Policy limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under Philippine law. Specific agreements may supplement or prevail under the document hierarchy.

1. Purpose and Scope

This Refund Policy explains how Operava Global Solutions Opc, organized under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, handles refund requests, cancellations, service credits, service-quality concerns, billing errors, payment disputes, and related matters. It applies to IT, BPO, staffing, SaaS, digital products, and related services. Consistent with applicable Philippine law; does not limit rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

2. Company and Service Overview

Operava is a Philippines-based IT and BPO company serving Clients in the Philippines and internationally. Operava does not provide services to offshore gaming activities.

3. Definitions

Defined terms include Agreement, Billing Period, BPO Services, Business Client, Cancellation, Chargeback, Client, Consumer, Defect, Deliverable, Digital Products, Digital Services, Force Majeure, Full Refund, IT Services, Milestone, Partial Refund, Payment, Project, Refund, SaaS, Service Credit, Service Deficiency, Services, Staffing Services, Subscription, and Third-Party Services.

4. General Refund Principles

4.1 No Unlawful Blanket Exclusion

Operava does not apply an unlawful blanket no-refund rule.

4.2 Mandatory Rights Preserved

Mandatory Philippine legal rights are preserved.

4.3 Cancellation Is Not Automatically a Refund

Cancellation does not automatically entitle Client to a Refund for services already performed or committed costs.

4.4 Case-Specific Assessment

Refund requests are assessed fairly based on facts, Agreement, and law.

4.5 Remedies May Differ

Remedies may include correction, reperformance, partial/full refund, or service credit.

5. Consumer and Business Client Transactions

Consumers receive mandatory consumer-protection rights. Business Clients are generally governed by the applicable Agreement.

6. Relationship With Other Agreements

Hierarchy: mandatory law → signed MSA/Service Agreement → SOW/Order Form → SLA → product-specific terms → this Policy → general Terms.

7. Refund Eligibility

Eligibility includes duplicate charges, billing errors, unauthorized payments, non-commencement after valid cancellation, material service deficiency not cured, non-conforming deliverables, express contractual refunds, prepaid unperformed services, or mandatory legal requirements.

8. Full Refunds

May apply for duplicated authorized payments, charges in error, inability to commence solely due to Operava’s material failure, cancellation before work begins, express contractual terms, mandatory legal rights, or reasonable good-faith determination.

9. Partial Refunds

May apply for prepaid periods partially unperformed, partially completed projects, partial service deficiencies, pro-rated recurring services where required, or fair resolution.

10. Service Credits and Alternative Remedies

Service Credits may be offered where legally permissible. Credits expire twelve (12) months after issue unless otherwise stated. Mandatory monetary remedies are not replaced by credits where law requires otherwise.

11. Software Development Refunds

Deposits and discovery fees compensate preliminary work. Fees for completed work and accepted milestones are generally not refundable for change of mind. Partially completed projects are valued by work performed, milestones, hours, deliverables, and third-party costs.

12. Web, Mobile and Platform Development Refunds

Assessed against written scope and acceptance criteria. Correctable defects may be remediated before refund consideration. Out-of-scope requests are change requests.

13. SaaS and Subscription Refunds

Cancellation generally stops future billing after the current billing period. Monthly fees for the current period are generally not refundable for non-use alone. Annual/prepaid early termination follows the Agreement and law. Duplicate billing and errors will be corrected.

14. IT Consulting and Professional Services

Fees are generally earned as services are performed. Client remains responsible for services properly performed upon cancellation after work begins.

15. Cloud, Hosting and Infrastructure Services

Unused capacity does not automatically create a refund. Interruptions follow the applicable SLA. Third-party infrastructure credits may depend on third-party recovery.

16. BPO Services

Fees for services properly performed are generally not refundable for later discontinuation alone. Prepaid balances consider work performed, reserved capacity, onboarding costs, notice, and law.

17. Staffing and Outsourcing Services

Fees for personnel time properly delivered are generally not refundable. Cancellation leaves obligations for services performed and notice-period fees under the Agreement.

18. Recruitment and RPO Services

Fees for recruitment work properly performed are generally not refundable for hiring-plan changes alone. Replacement guarantees follow the Agreement.

19. Data Processing and Back-Office Services

Operava-attributable deficiencies may be corrected, reprocessed, credited, or otherwise remedied. Client data issues do not create Operava refund liability unless Operava agreed to validate those inputs.

20. Digital Products and Digital Deliverables

After access or download, return may be limited by digital delivery nature, without excluding mandatory remedies for defects or billing errors.

21. Non-Refundable or Generally Non-Refundable Charges

Subject to law: services properly performed; completed milestones; documented hours; onboarding/setup properly performed; dedicated personnel and reserved capacity; consumed usage; non-recoverable third-party costs; taxes and processing fees where lawfully chargeable; lawful cancellation/notice charges.

22. Client Cancellation

Before work begins: refund assessed against work, capacity, and costs. After work begins: responsibility for services performed remains. Change of mind does not automatically create a refund.

23. Service Deficiencies and Defective Services

Report promptly with evidence. Operava investigates and may correct, reperform, replace personnel, credit, or refund as appropriate.

24. Client-Caused Issues

Refunds may not be available to the extent issues are caused by incomplete Client inputs, lack of cooperation, out-of-scope changes, Client infrastructure, misuse, or third-party services not controlled by Operava.

25. Third-Party Services and Costs

Third-party costs follow third-party terms. Operava is not required to refund non-refundable third-party costs unless law or Agreement requires.

26. Payment Processing and Currency Conversion

Refunds generally return via original payment method. Exchange-rate differences and intermediary fees are generally Client’s responsibility unless law requires otherwise.

27. Taxes and Refund Adjustments

Tax treatment of refunds follows applicable law. Credit notes or adjustments may be issued as required.

28. Refund Request Procedure

Submit to cs@operavaglobal.com, the Client portal at www.operavaglobal.com, or another confirmed channel. Provide identity, invoice/transaction references, payment details, explanation, and evidence. Submit within 30 days of awareness (administrative target, not a limit on longer legal periods).

29. Refund Review and Investigation

Operava aims to decide within 30 business days after receiving reasonably necessary information.

30. Refund Determination

Based on mandatory law, Agreement, authorization of payment, extent of performance, defects, cure opportunity, Client-caused issues, third-party costs, and taxes.

31. Refund Calculation

Illustrative: Refundable Amount = Amount Actually Paid − Value of Services Properly Performed − Properly Authorized Non-Recoverable Third-Party Costs − Other Lawful Contractual Charges.

32. Refund Processing

Target: within 15 business days after amount and method are finalized.

33. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

Contact Operava first where practicable. Unauthorized transactions should be reported promptly. Undisputed amounts remain payable. Abusive chargebacks may be contested with records.

34. Fraudulent or Abusive Refund Claims

Operava may investigate and respond to fraudulent or abusive claims, without treating good-faith complaints as abusive.

35. Service Interruptions

Assessed under the Agreement and SLA. Planned maintenance, Client-caused issues, third-party failures, and force majeure may affect eligibility without eliminating mandatory rights.

36. Force Majeure

Neither party is responsible for delay caused by force majeure, subject to Agreement and law.

37. International Clients

Policy designed principally under Philippine law; mandatory laws of a Client’s location may also apply.

38. Data Privacy

Personal data handled under applicable data-protection law including the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and Operava’s Privacy Policy.

39. Intellectual Property

Refund or cancellation does not by itself change IP ownership or licenses; governed by the Agreement.

40. Confidentiality and Security

Refund processing does not waive confidentiality or security obligations.

41. Limitation of Liability

Subject to the Agreement and mandatory law. Liability that cannot lawfully be limited is not excluded.

42. Complaints and Escalation

Level 1: cs@operavaglobal.com · https://support.operavaglobal.com
Level 2: cs-escalation2@operavaglobal.com
Level 3: Formal dispute resolution under the Agreement; consumers may also seek assistance from competent authorities including DTI where applicable.

43. Dispute Resolution

Good-faith negotiation first; mediation where appropriate; arbitration only if validly agreed; urgent relief and statutory remedies preserved.

44. Governing Law

Primarily the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, without prejudice to mandatory laws applicable to a particular transaction.

45. Policy Amendments

Published at www.operavaglobal.com/refundpolicy. Amendments do not retroactively remove accrued refund rights unless law permits and Client agrees.

46. Relationship With Other Policies and Agreements

Read with Terms, Privacy Policy, MSA, SOW, SLA, SaaS Terms, Staffing Agreement, and related documents.

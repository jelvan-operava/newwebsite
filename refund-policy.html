Refund Policy

Authoritative refund rules, cancellation conditions, service credit allocations, billing error remedies, and resolution frameworks.

Entity Operava Global Solutions Effective Date August 25, 2026 Jurisdiction Republic of the Philippines Framework 49 Authoritative Sections

Mandatory Compliance Notice. Cancellation does not automatically generate a cash refund. Nothing in this Policy limits, excludes, or modifies statutory rights and remedies that cannot lawfully be waived under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines. Effective August 25, 2026.

Related Documents: Terms and Conditions Privacy Policy Customer Support

1. Purpose and Scope

This Refund Policy (the “Policy”) explains how Operava Global Solutions OPC, an entity organized and operating under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines (“Operava,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), handles refund requests, cancellations, service credits, service-quality concerns, billing errors, payment disputes, and related contractual remedies.

This Policy governs all commercial arrangements under which Operava offers, sells, delivers, manages, or supports Technology Services, Business Process Outsourcing (BPO), dedicated staffing, SaaS platforms, digital deliverables, and consulting engagements.

2. Company and Service Overview

Operava is an SEC-registered Philippine corporation providing Information Technology and BPO solutions to domestic and international clients under a remote-first operating model. Operava conducts business strictly within the bounds permitted by Philippine and international law and does not provide services to offshore gaming activities.

3. Definitions

For the purposes of this Policy, the following definitions apply:

  • Agreement: The Master Services Agreement (MSA), Statement of Work (SOW), Order Form, or digital contract entered into between Operava and the Client.
  • Billing Period: The recurring cycle (monthly, milestone, or upfront) for which services are invoiced.
  • BPO Services: Business Process Outsourcing, customer experience, back-office data processing, and operational workflows.
  • Business Client: Commercial entities, corporate enterprises, or professional clients entering into commercial engagements.
  • Cancellation: Formal written notice by a Client terminating services prior to standard expiration.
  • Chargeback: A transaction dispute initiated directly through a credit card issuer, bank, or payment processor.
  • Defect / Material Deficiency: A verifiable failure of a deliverable to conform to documented, mutually agreed technical specifications.
  • Deliverable: Work product, code, documentation, reports, or assets produced and delivered under an SOW.
  • Service Credit: Non-cash credit applied toward future invoices or service hours with Operava.
  • Staffing Services: Dedicated engineering, customer service, or operational personnel assigned to a Client engagement.

4. General Refund Principles

  • 4.1 No Blanket Exclusion: Operava does not maintain an unlawful blanket “no refund” exclusion where consumer or contractual statutory rights apply.
  • 4.2 Mandatory Rights Preserved: Statutory rights under Republic Act No. 7394 (Consumer Act of the Philippines) and relevant commercial laws remain fully effective.
  • 4.3 Cancellation vs. Refund: Contract cancellation ends future obligations but does not automatically entitle the Client to a refund of work already properly executed.
  • 4.4 Case-Specific Assessment: Every claim is evaluated individually based on verified timesheets, server logs, code commits, and project acceptance milestones.
  • 4.5 Hierarchy of Remedies: Where a deficiency exists, the preferred remedy sequence is: (1) prompt correction/bug fix, (2) reperformance of service, (3) issuance of Service Credit, or (4) partial/full cash refund.

5. Consumer and Business Client Transactions

Where services are engaged by an individual for personal use, mandatory consumer-protection laws apply. Commercial B2B relationships with corporate entities are governed primarily by the signed Master Services Agreement, Statement of Work, and commercial terms.

6. Relationship With Other Agreements (Order of Precedence)

In the event of conflict, the following order of precedence controls: (a) mandatory statutory law; (b) mutually signed Master Services Agreement; (c) Statement of Work (SOW) or Order Form; (d) Service Level Agreement (SLA); (e) this Refund Policy; and (f) general website Terms.

7. Refund Eligibility Conditions

Refund claims are eligible for formal evaluation under the following verified circumstances:

  • Documented duplicate billing or computational invoicing errors.
  • Unauthorized or fraudulent payments verified by merchant security protocols.
  • Valid cancellation before any work, resource reservation, or technical environment setup has commenced.
  • Material service deficiency that Operava fails to cure within the contractually defined cure window (typically 14–30 business days).
  • Prepaid retainers where hours or milestones remain wholly unperformed upon lawful contract termination.

8. Full Refunds

Full refunds are granted strictly where payment was processed erroneously, unauthorized, or where a project was cancelled within lawful grace periods prior to the commencement of any work or team allocation.

9. Partial Refunds

Where work has begun, partial refunds are calculated by deducting the verified pro-rata value of properly performed services, accrued labor hours, and non-recoverable third-party commitments.

10. Service Credits & Validity

Service credits issued as commercial accommodations are non-transferable, non-cashable, and valid for twelve (12) months from the issuance date, after which they expire automatically unless extended in writing.

11. Custom Software & Systems Development

Software development follows phased milestone delivery. Once an engineering milestone is tested, demonstrated, and formally accepted (or deemed accepted under contract terms), payments allocated to that milestone are non-refundable.

12. Web, Mobile & Platform Engineering

Delivered source code and platform builds are backed by a standard 30-day warranty against undocumented critical bugs. Reported defects are remediated at Operava's expense; bugs do not warrant immediate cash refunds where prompt cure is provided.

13. SaaS & Recurring Subscriptions

Subscription fees for proprietary software tools or portal access are billed in advance. Cancellations take effect at the conclusion of the active billing cycle; mid-cycle pro-rata refunds are not issued except where statutory rights mandate.

14. IT Consulting & Advisory Services

Advisory hours, technical feasibility reviews, and architecture sessions delivered by senior technology executives represent time-expended expertise and are non-refundable once the consultation has concluded.

15. Cloud, Hosting & Infrastructure Support

Third-party cloud infrastructure charges (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, domain registrars) incurred on the Client’s behalf are completely non-recoverable and strictly non-refundable.

16. Business Process Outsourcing (BPO)

BPO engagements require reserved seat capacity, facilities, and dedicated staffing. Cancellation requires 30 days written notice to allow proper resource de-allocation. Unused prepaid agent hours are reconciled at contract termination.

17. Dedicated Staffing & Resource Augmentation

If an assigned specialist does not meet agreed performance benchmarks during the initial 14-day evaluation window, Operava provides an immediate replacement talent at no additional placement cost.

18. Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO)

Retained recruitment search fees cover sourcing, talent marketing, and multi-tier technical screening. In the event a placed candidate departs within the guaranteed placement warranty period, Operava provides a replacement search at zero additional fee.

19. Data Processing & Back-Office Operations

Volume-based data processing, document OCR, and verification workflows are billed based on processed records. Disputed batches are subject to sampling and quality auditing prior to any credit determination.

20. Digital Products & Deliverables

Completed digital deliverables, blueprints, architectural documentation, and custom software repositories downloaded by the Client are considered delivered and are non-refundable.

21. Non-Refundable Items & Categories

Except where mandated by law, the following are strictly non-refundable:

  • Completed work accepted by the Client or where the sign-off period has expired without written objection.
  • Discontinuation of services due to Client change of business strategy or change of mind.
  • Direct software licenses, API consumption, or third-party cloud costs purchased on Client behalf.
  • Initial technical setup, environment provisioning, and compliance onboarding fees.

22. Client-Initiated Cancellations

To cancel an active engagement, written notice must be submitted via email to cs@operavaglobal.com. The Client remains responsible for all fees and deliverables accrued through the effective termination date.

23. Service Deficiencies & Remediation

If a deliverable fails to meet contract specifications, the Client must furnish specific, reproducible evidence within ten (10) business days of delivery. Operava is entitled to a reasonable remediation window to correct non-conformances prior to any refund discussion.

24. Client-Caused Delays & Non-Cooperation

Operava is not liable, and refunds will not be issued, for project delays or deficiencies caused by delayed Client feedback, missing access credentials, third-party API outages outside Operava's control, or scope modifications.

25. Third-Party Costs & Pass-Through Expenses

All third-party disbursements (including cloud computing, telecommunications trunk lines, and commercial software licenses) are passed through at agreed rates and cannot be refunded once purchased.

26. Payment Processing & Currency Conversions

Approved refunds are processed in the original transaction currency (typically USD or PHP). Operava is not responsible for foreign exchange fluctuations or international intermediary banking wire fees imposed by the recipient bank.

27. Taxes & Statutory Deductions

Any Value Added Tax (VAT), withholding taxes, or statutory deductions remitted to the Philippine Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) will be accounted for in accordance with applicable tax regulations.

28. Refund Claim Submission Procedure

All refund requests must be formally submitted in writing to cs@operavaglobal.com with the subject line Refund Request — [Client / Account Name] — [Invoice #]. The request must include: (a) transaction date and payment proof; (b) relevant Statement of Work and invoice number; (c) detailed factual explanation of the grounds for refund; and (d) supporting evidence, logs, or communications.

29. Review & Investigation Timeline

Operava acknowledges claims within two (2) business days. An investigation into work logs, timesheets, and technical deliverables is completed within thirty (30) business days of submission.

30. Determination Factors

Claim assessments evaluate contractual SOW terms, Git repository commit logs, verified timesheets, communication records, defect severity, and the opportunity granted to Operava to remediate issues.

31. Calculation Formula

Where a partial refund is determined appropriate, the formula utilized is:

Refundable Amount = Total Amount Paid − Value of Properly Executed Services − Non-Recoverable Third-Party Disbursements − Lawful Administrative / Merchant Fees

32. Method & Timeline of Payment

Approved refunds are disbursed within fifteen (15) business days following formal written approval, utilizing the original method of payment (bank transfer, wire, or payment gateway).

33. Chargebacks & Inappropriate Payment Disputes

Clients agree to engage Operava's formal dispute resolution channels prior to initiating chargebacks. Unwarranted chargebacks that bypass this Policy are subject to billing administrative fees, immediate service suspension, and collection actions.

34. Fraudulent & Bad-Faith Claims

Claims containing fabricated information or intended to unjustly retain proprietary work product without payment will result in immediate termination of all accounts and referral to legal counsel.

35. Service Interruptions & Maintenance

Temporary downtime for routine maintenance or upstream ISP interruptions does not constitute a breach of contract and does not entitle the Client to refunds, provided reasonable notice is given.

36. Force Majeure & Uncontrollable Events

Neither party is deemed in default for delays or non-performance caused by acts of God, typhoons, severe power grid failures, warfare, civil disturbances, or government embargoes.

37. International Clients & Cross-Border Engagements

International clients are subject to cross-border wire rules. Approved refunds will reflect net funds received after standard cross-border banking fees.

38. Data Privacy & Confidentiality in Disputes

All documentation submitted during refund assessments is handled in strict compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and our Privacy Policy.

39. Intellectual Property in Refund Situations

If a deliverable is refunded in full, all licenses, code ownership, and usage rights granted to the Client are immediately revoked. The Client must permanently delete and cease all use of the unpurchased assets.

40. Limitation of Liability

Operava’s aggregate liability arising out of any refund claim or service dispute is strictly capped at the net fees actually paid by the Client for the specific deliverable giving rise to the claim.

41. Dispute Escalation Level 1 (Customer Support)

Submit claim to cs@operavaglobal.com for initial review and operational assessment by your dedicated account team.

42. Dispute Escalation Level 2 (Executive Operations)

If unresolved at Level 1, escalate in writing to cs-escalation2@operavaglobal.com for executive management review within ten (10) business days.

43. Dispute Escalation Level 3 (Formal Mediation & Arbitration)

Matters not resolved at Level 2 shall be submitted to confidential mediation in the Republic of the Philippines prior to instituting legal proceedings.

44. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

This Policy is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of the Philippines. The competent courts of the Philippines have exclusive jurisdiction over all disputes.

45. Amendments & Updates

Operava reserves the right to modify this Policy periodically. Updates take effect upon posting to this URL with an updated Effective Date. Ongoing use of services constitutes acceptance of revised terms.

46. Severability & Construction

If any clause of this Policy is deemed unenforceable by a court of competent jurisdiction, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

For inquiries, support tickets, and formal submissions:

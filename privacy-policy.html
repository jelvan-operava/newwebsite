Privacy Policy

Website visitors, job applicants, employees, clients, customers, and digital platform users. Comprehensive data privacy framework.

Entity Operava Global Solutions Effective Date August 17, 2026 Jurisdiction Republic of the Philippines (RA 10173) Contact compliance@operavaglobal.com

Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Operava Global Solutions is committed to upholding rigorous standards of transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality across all personal data processing activities.

Related Documents: Terms and Conditions Refund Policy Contact Compliance

1. Organizational Commitment

Operava Global Solutions ("Operava", "we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy and fundamental data rights of every individual. We collect only information reasonably necessary for identified and legitimate commercial purposes, handle data lawfully, safeguard information through technical and organizational controls, and retain it strictly for periods justified by law or business necessity.

2. Who This Policy Applies To

This Policy applies to visitors of our websites, applicants seeking employment or contract placement, active employees and contractors, clients and prospective commercial partners, customer service end-users, and individuals utilizing our digital applications. Specific processing activities may be governed by supplemental project notices or data processing agreements (DPAs).

3. Information We Collect

  • Identification & Contact: Full legal name, business title, email address, telephone numbers, and corporate mailing addresses.
  • Government & Regulatory Identifiers: Tax identification numbers, identity documentation, or work permits where lawfully required for payroll, statutory reporting, or compliance.
  • Professional & Career History: Resumes/CVs, educational credentials, certifications, technical skill assessments, interview evaluations, employment references, and work preferences.
  • Commercial & Billing Records: Company information, authorized signatories, billing addresses, purchase orders, milestone receipts, and payment logs.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP addresses, browser specifications, operating system details, device fingerprints, access timestamps, referral URLs, and audit logs recorded during service interactions.

4. Sensitive Personal Information

Sensitive personal information is processed exclusively when an explicit lawful basis exists under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, or with explicit data subject consent. We urge candidates and visitors not to submit unrequested sensitive personal data (such as health details or political opinions) through public submission channels.

5. Methods of Data Collection

We collect information directly from you when you complete forms on our website, submit your resume, schedule an interview, or execute a Statement of Work. Additional data may be received through authorized client representatives, third-party verification providers, or automated telemetry logs upon visiting our platforms.

6. Legitimate Processing Purposes

Personal data is processed to facilitate:

  • Delivery of IT engineering, cloud solutions, and Business Process Outsourcing engagements.
  • Talent acquisition, AI-assisted candidate screening, technical evaluation, and assignment matching.
  • Customer support, incident response, ticket routing, and SLA tracking.
  • Invoicing, payment processing, tax compliance, and commercial auditing.
  • Platform cybersecurity, DDoS mitigation, intrusion detection, and access control.
  • Legal compliance under Philippine regulatory bodies and dispute resolution.

7. AI-Assisted Recruitment & Evaluation

Operava leverages automated and AI-assisted tooling to screen candidate resumes against role requirements, summarize applicant portfolios, and schedule technical interviews. AI tools are utilized as advisory aids for preliminary organization; critical hiring decisions and offers undergo human review by our talent acquisition team. Participation in screening does not create an employment obligation.

8. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under RA 10173 and international privacy frameworks, our processing relies on: (a) explicit data subject consent; (b) contractual necessity to deliver requested services; (c) compliance with statutory obligations under Philippine law; or (d) legitimate commercial interests that do not override personal fundamental rights.

9. Client Data & Outsourced Processing (Processor Role)

In BPO and managed services contracts, Operava frequently acts as a Data Processor on behalf of our enterprise Clients (who serve as Data Controllers). We process end-user data strictly under contractual instructions, strict confidentiality agreements, and bilateral Data Processing Agreements (DPAs).

10. Information Sharing & Disclosure

Operava does not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties. Information is disclosed solely to authorized internal personnel, trusted cloud hosting providers (e.g., AWS, GCP), vetted third-party payment gateways, legal counsel, and government authorities when compelled by lawful subpoena or court order.

11. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Because Operava operates a global, remote-first delivery model, personal information may be accessed or transferred across international borders. All cross-border transfers adhere to recognized international data transfer frameworks and standard contractual clauses to ensure parity of protection.

12. Security Safeguards

We maintain comprehensive administrative, physical, and technical safeguards. These include TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication, endpoint protection, and routine vulnerability audits.

13. Security Incident & Breach Management

In accordance with NPC Circular 16-03, Operava maintains an active Incident Response Protocol. In the event of a verified security incident affecting sensitive personal data, we will notify affected individuals and the National Privacy Commission (NPC) within statutory deadlines.

14. Data Retention & Disposal

Personal data is retained only for the duration necessary to satisfy the commercial or statutory purposes for which it was gathered. Candidate profiles are maintained for active recruitment cycles; tax and financial logs are held in accordance with Philippine BIR statutory mandates (minimum 5–10 years). Upon expiration, records are securely scrubbed or anonymized.

15. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We employ essential session cookies to maintain platform security, preserve user preferences, and record traffic analytics. Visitors can manage or disable non-essential cookies via their browser configuration settings without compromising core website functionality.

16. Candidate & Applicant Rights

Job applicants may request an update or withdrawal of their candidate files at any time. Submitting an application registers interest in our talent pool but does not constitute an offer or guarantee of permanent assignment.

17. Employee & Contractor Data

Personnel records are gathered for payroll administration, benefits provision, performance monitoring, and statutory reporting under DOLE, SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG regulations.

18. Protection of Minors

Operava does not knowingly solicit or collect information from children under the age of 18. If we discover that a minor's information has been collected without parental consent, we take immediate measures to delete that data from our production systems.

19. Data Subject Rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, individuals hold fundamental rights regarding their personal data:

  • Right to be Informed: To know whether personal data is being processed, and the details of such processing.
  • Right to Access: To request reasonable access to your personal records in our custody.
  • Right to Rectification: To dispute inaccuracies and have incomplete or erroneous information corrected.
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: To request suspension, withdrawal, or destruction of unauthorized or outdated data.
  • Right to Object: To withhold consent or object to automated profiling or marketing communications.
  • Right to Data Portability: To obtain an electronic copy of personal data in a standard, machine-readable format.
  • Right to File a Complaint: To lodge formal complaints before the National Privacy Commission (NPC).
  • Right to Indemnification: To seek damages for verified violations of statutory privacy rights.

20. How to Exercise Your Privacy Rights

To exercise any of the rights enumerated above, submit a formal written request to our Data Protection Officer at compliance@operavaglobal.com. Please provide your full name, relationship with Operava, and a clear description of the records in question to enable identity verification.

22. Client / Processor Coordination

Where Operava acts as a data processor for a commercial Client, privacy requests concerning that Client’s end-user data will be forwarded to the respective Client controller for authoritative instruction.

23. Third-Party Websites & Integrations

Our website may include links to external services, payment gateways, or client tools. We encourage users to review the privacy notices of external websites, as Operava is not responsible for independent third-party privacy practices.

24. Marketing & Communications

We do not send unsolicited commercial spam. Any marketing emails or talent updates provide a direct, one-click opt-out link. Operational notifications regarding billing and active projects remain unaffected by marketing preferences.

25. Data Accuracy & Integrity

We take reasonable steps to ensure that personal information in our possession remains accurate, complete, and current. Data subjects are invited to inform us of changes to their contact details or professional status.

26. Organizational Accountability

Operava enforces strict confidentiality obligations across all employees and contractors, conducts mandatory privacy awareness training, and enforces formal disciplinary consequences for privacy policy violations.

27. Privacy by Design

Our engineering teams incorporate Privacy by Design into all systems architecture, minimizing data payloads, segregating database tenants, and utilizing pseudonymization where appropriate.

28. Policy Updates & Amendments

We may amend this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological advancements or legislative updates. Changes will be posted to this page with an updated Effective Date.

29. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, in particular Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

30. Compliance Documentation

Operava maintains internal records of processing activities, risk assessments, and privacy impact evaluations as mandated for Philippine SEC-registered organizations.

31. Core Privacy Principles

Transparency

Individuals have the right to know how their personal data is collected, stored, and utilized.

Legitimate Purpose

Processing must be strictly tied to explicit, lawful, and declared business requirements.

Proportionality

Only data strictly relevant and necessary to the purpose shall be collected and processed.

Security

Rigorous organizational, physical, and technical safeguards prevent unauthorized disclosure.

Accountability

Operava remains fully responsible for demonstrating compliance with privacy regulations.

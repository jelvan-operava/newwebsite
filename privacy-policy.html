Privacy Policy

Website, applicants, employees, clients, customers, and service users. Effective 17 August 2026.

Effective 17 August 2026. Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Review against actual processing activities before relying on this notice for compliance decisions.

Entity: OPERAVA Global Solutions · Contact: compliance@operavaglobal.com · Terms · Refund Policy

1. Commitment

OPERAVA respects privacy and seeks to collect only information reasonably necessary for identified and legitimate purposes, use it appropriately, protect it through reasonable safeguards, and retain it only as long as necessary or legally required.

2. Who this applies to

Website visitors, job applicants, employees, contractors, clients, customers, vendors, and digital-platform users. Specific processing may have separate notices.

3. Information we may collect

  • Name and contact details
  • Address and identification where necessary and lawful
  • Resume/CV, education, employment history, skills, certifications, references
  • Interview and assessment information; availability and work preferences
  • Business, client, contract, and billing information
  • IP address, browser/device data, timestamps, website activity, cookies, security/audit logs

4. Sensitive information

Processed only where there is a lawful basis and appropriate safeguards. Do not submit sensitive information unless requested through a secure process.

5. Collection methods

Directly from individuals, forms, applications, interviews, email, contracts, digital platforms, authorized representatives, service providers, or lawful public sources. Some technical data is collected automatically.

6. Purposes

Communications; service delivery; client management; recruitment and AI-assisted screening; employment; workforce operations; customer service; BPO; billing; IT and security; quality assurance; training; analytics; legal compliance; dispute resolution; protection of rights and systems.

7. AI-assisted recruitment

May process applicant information for screening, communication, scheduling, or assessment. AI-assisted screening does not create employment. Human review may occur in later stages.

8. Lawful basis

Consent, contract necessity, legal obligations, legitimate interests where permitted, or another lawful basis under applicable law.

9. Client data / outsourced processing

OPERAVA may process personal information on behalf of clients for BPO, IT, and related services under contractual instructions.

10. Disclosure

To authorized personnel, clients, service providers, professional advisers, auditors, and authorities where necessary and lawful. OPERAVA does not authorize unauthorized sale of personal information.

11. International processing

As a remote-first provider, information may be processed across jurisdictions where lawful and necessary, with appropriate safeguards.

12–14. Security, incidents, retention

Reasonable organizational, physical, and technical safeguards. Incident response procedures apply. Information is retained only as reasonably necessary, then securely deleted or anonymized.

15. Cookies

May be used for essential operation, security, preferences, analytics, and functionality. Browser settings can restrict cookies.

16–18. Applicants, employees, minors

Applicant data supports evaluation and recruitment only — application does not guarantee employment. Employee data supports administration and assignment. OPERAVA does not intentionally seek children’s data where inappropriate.

19. Data subject rights

  • Right to be informed
  • Right to access
  • Right to correct/rectify
  • Right to object
  • Right to erasure or blocking where applicable
  • Right to data portability where applicable
  • Right to lodge a complaint
  • Right to damages where provided by law

20–22. Requests & contact

Provide enough information to identify the requester. OPERAVA may verify identity. Processor requests for client data may need to go to the client/controller.

Privacy contact: Operava Compliance Team · compliance@operavaglobal.com

23–28. Third parties, marketing, accuracy, accountability, privacy by design, changes

Third parties have their own practices. Marketing follows applicable opt-out rules. OPERAVA seeks accurate data and treats privacy as an organizational responsibility. Material changes will be communicated appropriately.

29. Governing law

Philippine privacy law, including RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), its IRR, and NPC issuances. Additional jurisdiction-specific requirements may apply.

30. Compliance note

This policy does not by itself establish every registration or authorization that might apply. OPERAVA should maintain internal privacy documentation appropriate to actual processing.

31. Privacy principles

Transparency

People should understand what is processed and why.

Legitimate purpose

Processing for identified and lawful purposes.

Proportionality

Only information reasonably necessary.

Security

Appropriate protection of personal information.

Accountability

OPERAVA remains responsible within its role.
